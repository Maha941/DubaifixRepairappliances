## 1. THE 6 CORE DUBAI REPAIR SERVICES

Every website built under this skill must showcase these 6 standardized local services with specific diagnostic symptoms, parts handled, and AED price transparency:

### 1. Washing Machine Repair & Servicing
- **Common Symptoms**: Machine not draining, heavy drum vibration/banging, error code on display, spin cycle failure, water leaking from bottom, door lock jam.
- **Components Repaired**: Drain pump, motor carbon brushes, drive belt, shock absorbers, water inlet solenoid valve, main PCB control board.
- **Starting Price Indicator**: *From AED 120 (Diagnostic & Minor Fix)*

### 2. Refrigerator & Chiller Repair
- **Common Symptoms**: Fridge not cooling while freezer works, ice buildup on back panel, clicking sound from compressor, water leakage inside trays, bad odor / gas smell.
- **Components Repaired**: Compressor replacement, R134a/R600a eco-gas recharge, defrost thermostat, inverter PCB board, condenser fan motor, door gasket seal.
- **Starting Price Indicator**: *From AED 150 (Free Inspection with Repair)*

### 3. Dishwasher Repair & Descaling
- **Common Symptoms**: Dishes coming out dirty or wet, water not draining after cycle, E15 / E24 error codes, leaking from front door, foul drainage smell.
- **Components Repaired**: Circulation wash pump, drain impeller, heating element, float switch, spray arms, electronic timer.
- **Starting Price Indicator**: *From AED 130 (Same-Day Doorstep Service)*

### 4. AC Repair & Deep Chemical Maintenance
- **Critical Dubai Need**: Extreme summer heat up to 50°C makes rapid AC response a critical emergency service.
- **Common Symptoms**: AC blowing warm room-temperature air, water dripping from indoor unit, foul moldy odor, noisy blower fan, high DEWA electrical bills.
- **Components Repaired**: Compressor capacitor, refrigerant gas top-up (R410A / R22), coil pressure chemical wash, drainage tray declogging, PCB sensor replacement.
- **Starting Price Indicator**: *From AED 150 (Deep Clean & Gas Check)*

### 5. Cooking Range, Oven & Stove Repair
- **Common Symptoms**: Burners not igniting, oven not reaching set baking temperature, uneven flame/yellow flame, glass top crack, gas safety valve trip.
- **Components Repaired**: Spark ignition plug, safety thermocouple, oven bake element, gas thermostat valve, ventilation fan, heavy-duty hinges.
- **Starting Price Indicator**: *From AED 140 (Certified Gas & Electric Technicians)*

### 6. Clothes Dryer Repair (Tumble / Condenser)
- **Common Symptoms**: Dryer tumbling without heat, clothes taking 3+ hours to dry, loud squealing belt noise, drum won't rotate, overheating error.
- **Components Repaired**: Heating element assembly, drive belt & idler pulley, high-limit thermal fuse, moisture sensor, lint duct deep cleaning.
- **Starting Price Indicator**: *From AED 130 (Original Factory Replacement Parts)*

## 2.1 MANDATORY UNIVERSAL BUTTON CONSISTENCY STANDARD
All buttons across all pages and sections MUST strictly obey these rules:

1. **Shape Consistency**:
   - Every single action button MUST use the **Pill Shape** (`border-radius: 9999px;`).
   - Strictly NO square, rectangular, or 6px/8px corners on buttons.
2. **Size & Typography Consistency**:
   - Primary and secondary buttons across sections must have consistent uniform sizing:
     `padding: 13px 28px;` (clamp `12px 24px` to `14px 30px` on mobile), font-weight: `700`, font-size: `0.95rem` to `1rem`, display: `inline-flex`, align-items: `center`, justify-content: `center`, gap: `10px`.
3. **Canonical Signature Red (`#e50914`)**:
   - Whenever red is used on a button, it MUST strictly be `#e50914`.
   - Never use alternate reds like `#c80812`, `#cc0812`, or `#d0080a`.
4. **2-Button Color Pairing Rule**:
   - Whenever 2 buttons appear together (Hero, About, Diagnostic tabs, Fleet, FAQ, Banners, Modals):
     - **Button 1 (Primary)**: Solid Red Pill (`#e50914`) with bold white text.
     - **Button 2 (Secondary)**: Section-Adaptive Pill (Crisp White `#ffffff` with subtle border/dark text on dark sections, or crisp white/dark glass on light sections), with matching height and pill shape.
5. **Hero Double-Shutter Sliding Door Animation**:
   - All buttons must feature the signature hero dual-shutter animation:
     - `::before` (left shutter, slides from left to center `translateX(-102%) -> translateX(0)` on hover).
     - `::after` (right shutter, slides from right to center `translateX(102%) -> translateX(0)` on hover).
     - Hover elevation lift: `transform: translateY(-2px);`.
     - Arrow glide on hover: `transform: translateX(4px);` on `.btn-arrow` or SVG.
     - Click active press: `transform: translateY(1px) scale(0.98);`.
     - Isolation: All text and icons must be on `z-index: 2` with `position: relative` so sliding shutters glide underneath without obscuring text.
