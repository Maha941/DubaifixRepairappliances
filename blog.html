AC Repair & Maintenance

5 Critical Signs Your AC Needs Immediate Chemical Coil Washing in Dubai

Sep 16, 2026 • 5 Min Read • By Senior HVAC Engineer

Operating air conditioners in Dubai during summer is demanding. Dust storms, humidity, and 45°C+ ambient temperatures cause fine sand particles to mix with moisture inside your split or central AC indoor evaporator coil. Over time, this creates a thick sludge that chokes your cooling system.

1. Reduced Airflow & Weak Cooling

If your AC fan is running at full speed but blowing lukewarm air, dust buildup on the cooling fins is blocking thermal heat exchange. A standard filter rinse won't remove sticky grime inside the coils.

2. Unpleasant Musty Odors

Stagnant water trapped in dirty coils breeds bacteria and mold. When your AC starts emitting a damp, sour smell, it signals micro-organism growth inside the drain tray and evaporator unit.

3. Spike in DEWA Electricity Bills

A clogged coil forces the AC compressor to run twice as long to reach set thermostat temperatures. This unnecessary power consumption often increases monthly utility costs by 30% to 40%.

4. Water Dripping from the Indoor Unit

As condensation cannot drain smoothly through dust-choked drain pipes, water overflows from the internal drip tray, causing potential wall paint damage and electrical short-circuit risks.

5. Ice Formation on Copper Pipes

Restricted airflow prevents refrigerant gas from absorbing heat properly, causing the copper suction pipes to freeze over into solid ice blocks.

How to Fix Washing Machine Drum Noise & Drain Pump Blockage at Home

Sep 14, 2026 • 4 Min Read • By Master Appliance Specialist

Front-load and top-load washing machines are essential daily workhorses in Dubai homes. When your machine starts vibrating violently or fails to drain water, quick action prevents total motor or drum bearing failure.

Step 1: Clearing the Drain Pump Coin Trap

Located at the bottom front right of most machines, the coin filter traps stray coins, hairpins, and lint. Turn off power, place a shallow dish below the filter door, unscrew counter-clockwise, and flush out trapped debris.

Step 2: Diagnosing Excessive Drum Vibration

If your machine rattles during the high-speed spin cycle:

  • Verify all 4 leveling feet rest firmly on flat tiled floor.
  • Ensure transit bolts installed during moving have been removed.
  • Avoid overloading heavy blankets or towels to one side of the drum.

Step 3: Common Error Codes (E20, OE, UE, 4E)

Error codes on Samsung, LG, Bosch, or Siemens washers indicate specific faults. E20/OE indicates drainage pump timeout, while UE signifies an unbalanced load distribution inside the tub.

Why Is Your Refrigerator Not Cooling Properly? Dubai Summer Troubleshooting Guide

Sep 12, 2026 • 6 Min Read • By Certified Refrigeration Engineer

A failing refrigerator in Dubai's heat can lead to hundreds of Dirhams in spoiled groceries within hours. Understanding the most common cooling failure points allows fast action before total compressor failure.

1. Dusty Condenser Coils at Back/Bottom

Dust accumulation on rear black condenser coils prevents heat from escaping the refrigerant gas. Vacuuming these coils twice a year keeps your compressor operating smoothly at full cooling capacity.

2. Worn Out Rubber Door Gaskets

If warm air enters the fridge, moisture condenses into thick frost while cooling drops. Place a paper note between the door seal and close it — if it slides out easily without resistance, your magnetic gasket needs replacement.

3. Evaporator Fan Motor & Defrost Timer Failure

If your freezer is icy cold but the fresh food section below remains warm, a faulty evaporator fan or stuck defrost timer is preventing cold air from circulating downward.

4. Refrigerant Gas Leakage (R600a / R134a)

If the compressor runs continuously with a humming sound but neither the freezer nor fresh food section gets cold, your system has suffered a micro gas leak requiring pressure testing and recharging.
